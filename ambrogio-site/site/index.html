Ambrogio

One binary that supervises a team of resident agents, organises their work in projects and queues, and keeps the trace of what is done.

Ambrogio runs the always-on agent that manages the lab, and it can bring up other agents on demand — each with a personality written for the job it was given. Work is opened as issues in a project, handed to an agent, and carried to the end; every step leaves a report and a short brief beside it. Nothing is lost on a restart: the queue is durable, and what was handed over but not finished comes back.

  • What it does

    Projects, issues, documents, channels and the durable queue that carries work to the agents.

  • How it works

    One binary, one data directory, resident agent processes over the Agent Client Protocol.
