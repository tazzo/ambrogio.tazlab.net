Personal Agent Orchestrator

Ambrogio

Ambrogio keeps the trace of what is done. It gives order and documentation to every movement, and its own internal agents keep it correct.

Ambrogio 0.3 — The Butler

In 0.3 a queue owns a branch and a working directory of its own, the engine lands the work itself — a push, and a pull request — and every agent works inside a cage.

It is a personal tool. It is one static Go binary beside one SQLite file — no server to install, no database to run, no infrastructure, no team — and the operator runs it on his own machine and drives it from a page the binary serves itself. One person, or a small team, is the most it is made for.

What it does is orchestration: several agents working at the same time on the same things. Ambrogio supervises resident agent processes over the Agent Client Protocol, hands them work without two agents in one place, and exposes the whole of it — the queues, the steps, the documents, the verdicts — through one HTTP API and one event stream. It opens tasks of its own as well, the smaller jobs, and the agents it spawns carry them.

What it keeps is the trace: what was done, by whom and when — the operator's own work, the work of other tools, and the work of the agents Ambrogio spawns for itself. A queue says the order, its documents say why, and the record stays in the one file beside the binary.

That is the problem it removes. For one person the work grows past what one person can hold — many terminals open at once, documents written here and there, no answer to who did what and when — and Ambrogio is the one place where the work is written down, the documents stay, and the order of it can be read back afterwards.

It is not a professional product. It is a small thing that does its job well.

The four things worth knowing

  • The queues, and who accepts them

    Work is a queue with a declared ceiling, and nothing starts by itself: the operator accepts it.

  • The two doors, and the sandbox

    One door for the operator and a smaller one for the agents, and a worker that may only touch what its role allows.

  • The agents, the budget and the silence

    Every agent has a slot, a declared budget and a clock: a step that goes quiet is stopped, and what it did is kept.

  • The conversation

    A chat is a queue you talk to: its steps are the messages, its transcript is the chain, and it waits instead of closing.
